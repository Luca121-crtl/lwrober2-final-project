Compare Student Apartments

This page summarizes three example student communities to show how a simple housing hub could help students compare options. Distances and prices are sample values for this class project.

Sample Comparison of Greystar-Managed Student Apartments
Property City / Distance to Campus Starting Rent (per month) Bed Options Key Features
Maplewood Student Apartments Tempe, AZ – ~0.5 miles from campus $1,250+ 1–3 bedroom Study lounges, on-site fitness, close walk to campus
Riverside Hall Austin, TX – ~1.0 mile from campus $1,150+ Studio–2 bedroom In-unit laundry, modern interiors, bus route access
Highland Commons Tempe, AZ – ~0.8 miles from campus $1,100+ 2–4 bedroom Furnished units, high-speed internet, group study rooms

In a real housing hub, this table could be filtered by budget, distance from campus, or amenities to help students quickly narrow down which apartments fit their needs.
