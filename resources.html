Student Renter Resources

Renting your first apartment can feel confusing, especially if you’ve never signed a lease before. These resources are meant to help students think about budget, timing, and what to look for when choosing housing.

Before You Start Searching

  • Set a realistic monthly budget (rent + utilities + parking).
  • Decide if you want roommates or your own space.
  • Think about commute time and access to campus or transit.

Understanding Your Lease

  • Check start and end dates carefully.
  • Look for additional fees (parking, pets, utilities, admin).
  • Know what happens if you need to break the lease early.

Moving In and Living There

  • Do a move-in checklist and report issues right away.
  • Set up internet and utilities if they aren’t included.
  • Save maintenance contact info somewhere easy to find.
