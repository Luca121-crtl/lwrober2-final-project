Student Renter Resources

Renting your first apartment is a big step, especially when you are also balancing classes, work, and everything else that comes with student life. These tips are meant to help students understand what to look for before signing a lease at a Greystar-managed community or any other apartment.

Budget & Planning

  • Write out a realistic monthly budget, including rent, utilities, parking, and groceries.
  • Leave some space for unexpected costs like repairs, books, or medical expenses.
  • Compare the cost of living on campus vs. off campus before committing.

What to Ask Before You Apply

  • Is Wi-Fi included in the rent, or is it a separate bill?
  • Are units furnished, or will you need to bring your own furniture?
  • What are the quiet hours and guest policies in the community?

Understanding Your Lease

  • Check the start and end dates so they line up with your school schedule.
  • Look closely at fees (parking, pets, utilities, application fees, etc.).
  • Ask how renewals work if you plan to stay more than one year.

Helpful External Resources

  • Greystar Official Website – explore real communities and amenities.
  • ASU University Housing – general housing information for ASU students.
  • Federal Student Aid Resources – help with budgeting and financial planning.
